## It does not make airworthiness decisions

The airworthiness status, due dates, AD applicability, life limits and projections are worked out only from the records entered into the app, and those records or the app's rules may be incomplete, out of date or wrong. AD searches can miss directives, and AI transcription of logbooks can misread them. Always check against the aircraft's actual records, the current ADs and the manufacturer's data. Before each flight the pilot in command decides whether the aircraft is airworthy (14 CFR 91.7), and the owner or operator is responsible for maintaining it (14 CFR 91.403).
