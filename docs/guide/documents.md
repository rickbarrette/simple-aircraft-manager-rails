# Documents

The **Documents** tab keeps scans and files: registration, airworthiness certificate, insurance, logbook scans, receipts, 337s, reports.

- **+ Upload Document**: a name, type, collection, description, and one or more files (images, PDFs or text). Each file is a page in the viewer.
- **Bulk upload**: many files at once. Each becomes its own document named after the file; files with the same name are combined, in order, as pages of one document.
- **+ Collection** groups documents (for example *Engine logbook* or *Annual 2026*).

## Expiry

Give a registration, insurance policy or similar an expiration date and it shows on the airworthiness status as it approaches. An expired registration grounds the aircraft (91.203(a)(2)).

## Share-link visibility

Each collection or document is **Private**, visible on **all share links**, or **maintenance only** share links. See [Access, sharing and transfer](sharing.md).

## Legacy records

Tick **Legacy record** for scans of the paper logbooks and records from before the aircraft's *electronic records since* date. The paper originals remain the record for that period and must be kept.
