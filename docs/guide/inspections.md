# Inspections

The **Inspections** tab shows each required inspection, when it was last done and when it's next due.

## Setting up

**More → Add standard inspections** adds the recurring inspections and checks Part 91 calls for (annual, 100-hour, pitot-static and altimeter, transponder, ELT…). Pick the ones that apply to how the aircraft is flown; each comes with its interval and what being overdue means, and you can change them afterwards.

**+ Inspection Type** adds your own, for example a recurring inspection from an STC's instructions for continued airworthiness. Each type has:

- **Recurring** every so many hours and/or days, months or years (months and years are calendar months, due at the end of the month).
- What overdue means: **grounds the aircraft**, **no IFR flight** (91.411), **transponder can't be used** (91.413), or **advisory only**.
- **Overrun allowance**: for the 100-hour, 10 hours. Done late within it, the next one counts from when it was due (91.409(b)).
- **Also counts as**: an annual also counts as the 100-hour.

## Recording an inspection

**Record** next to an inspection logs the date and hours it was done, optionally linking the logbook entry. The [logbook](logbook.md) entry is the signed record (14 CFR 43.11); write it too.
