# Major repairs, alterations and weight & balance

## Major records

The **Major Records** tab lists major repairs and alterations: **+ Repair** or **+ Alteration** records the date, aircraft hours, what was done and by whom, the component, the Form 337 document, the logbook entry, and for an STC its number, holder and document.

If it includes **instructions for continued airworthiness**, tick it and use **Add the ICA's recurring inspection** to track it as an [inspection](inspections.md). If it changed the empty weight or CG, **Record the new W&B**.

## Weight & balance

The **W&B** tab keeps the history of empty weight and CG.

- **+ New W&B**: date, reason, empty weight, empty CG (arm aft of the datum), max gross weight, the alteration that caused it and the W&B report document. The newest record is the current one.
- **Loading setup**: from the POH, the stations (seats, baggage, fuel tanks) with their arms and maximums, the fuel weight per gallon, and the corners of the CG envelope. Enter the envelope either as CG in inches aft of the datum or as moment in lb-in/1000 (as POH moment charts and Garmin Pilot show it, e.g. 2300 lb at 104.5): pick which under **Envelope values are**. Moments are converted to CG (moment × 1000 ÷ weight) and the CG is shown next to each point.
- **Loading calculator**: enter people, baggage and fuel to see takeoff and landing weight and CG against max gross and the envelope.

The calculator is a planning aid from the figures entered. Check against the POH and the aircraft's current W&B report.
