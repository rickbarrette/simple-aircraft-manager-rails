# Oil, fuel and oil analysis

## Oil & Fuel

Oil and fuel added when logging a flight are recorded automatically. The **Oil & Fuel** tab also takes records on their own (**+ Add Oil** / **+ Add Fuel**): quantity added, level after, product and tach time.

It charts consumption and shows averages over the last 20 intervals, ignoring outliers. The records list shows the latest 50; older ones load as you scroll down (or with **Load more**). Tick **Exclude from averages** for an oil change, so the oil added isn't counted as consumption.

## Oil analysis

**Oil & Fuel → Oil analysis** tracks lab results and charts wear metals over time.

- **Import report**: upload an AvLab or Blackstone PDF. Every sample on it, including earlier ones, is added unless it's already there.
- **+ Add Oil Analysis**: enter results by hand.

Look for a steady trend rather than a single reading. Lead is left off the chart: it comes from 100LL and reads in the thousands.
