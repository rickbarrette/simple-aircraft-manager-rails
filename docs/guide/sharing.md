# Access, sharing and transfer

The **Access & Sharing** tab is for the aircraft's owners.

## People

Add people by email as **owner** or **pilot** (they need an account; ask the administrator). Change or remove their role at any time. See the roles table in [Getting started](getting-started.md).

## Share links

**Create Share Link** gives a read-only link that needs no account, for a mechanic, partner, buyer or insurer:

- **Current Status**: airworthiness, open squawks, components, inspection and AD status, consumption, notes and shared documents.
- **Maintenance Detail**: also the logbook, inspection and AD compliance history, major repairs and alterations, resolved squawks and the printable status report.

Give it a label and optionally an expiry. Documents only appear if their visibility allows it (see [Documents](documents.md)). Delete a link to revoke it. An aircraft can have up to 10.

## Records package

**Download records package** builds a zip for the FAA, a buyer or a new maintainer: every logbook entry as a PDF with its signatures, a signature register, the change history, the status report, the current recordkeeping procedures, and the data archive.

## Backup and transfer

**Download .sam.zip** exports the whole aircraft with its files. Anyone can load it with **Fleet → Import** (in this app or the original Simple Aircraft Manager) to make a copy they own, for example when the aircraft is sold. Use a new tail number if the original is already in that app.

## Activity

The **Activity** tab lists everything done on the aircraft and by whom. **Record history** shows every change to the maintenance records field by field (see [Electronic records and signing](records.md)).
