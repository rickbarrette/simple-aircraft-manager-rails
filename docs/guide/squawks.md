# Squawks

A squawk is a problem found with the aircraft. Anyone on the aircraft can report one.

## Report a squawk

**Report Squawk** asks for the issue, a priority, optionally the component, attachments (photos, PDFs) and notes.

| Priority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ground Aircraft** | The aircraft isn't airworthy until it's resolved. |
| **Fix Soon** | Fix before it gets worse. |
| **Fix at Next Inspection** | Can wait for the next inspection. |
| **Fix Eventually** | Minor. |

## Defer an inoperative item (91.213(d))

For inoperative equipment that isn't required, **Defer** records the decision. You confirm each condition of 14 CFR 91.213(d): not required by the type certificate, equipment list, 91.205 or another rule, nor by an AD; removed or deactivated and placarded *Inoperative*; and that a pilot or mechanic has determined it isn't a hazard. You record who decided and in which role. A deferred squawk no longer grounds the aircraft. **Undo deferral** (owners) reverses it.

## Resolve a squawk

Owners choose **Resolve**. You can record the fix as a logbook entry at the same time: the date, logbook, airframe total time, work performed and who signed it off. A resolved squawk can be **Reopen**ed.
